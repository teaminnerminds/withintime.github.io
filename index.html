The Story

Six months remain before the fate of two worlds is sealed and humanity as it's known ceases to exist. Yunis Caelum leads the charge to save humanity, followed closely by his ensemble of childhood friends — Estella Kisiel, Angie Alfaro, and Rictor Fitzsimmons — as they venture into Iolade, a world loosely connected to their own through a force unbeknownst to them.

With hopes of gaining answers to June's unprecedented disappearance just a year prior, the unlikely group of heroes make their way through Iolade, piecing together their larger roles in this prophecy as they aim to put an end to the Scion of Time's terror and allow both Iolade and Terrus to live in peace.

Utilising a fully unique turn-based combat system, Within Time throws you into the world of Iolade as you fight, talk and plan your way through each advesary that dare cross your path. Picking up to four active characters to fight as in combat you'll utilise the STANCE system to navigate tough encounters and achieve victory in the toughest spots.

With each of the thirteen playable characters having their own element to utilise, the possibilities for combat are near endless. Experience a wholy unique take on the turn-based RPG genre in Within Time.
