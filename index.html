Six months remain before the fate of two worlds is sealed, and humanity as it is known ceases to exist.

Yunis Caelum leads the charge, followed closely by his childhood friends Estella Kisiel, Angie Alfaro and Rictor Fitzsimmons.

Together they venture into Iolade, a world bound to their own Terrus by a force none of them understand.

They came looking for answers to June Clavis' disappearance. What they find is a prophecy, their place in it, and the terror of the Scion of Time.

01 Three years ago

She vanished

On February 5th, June Clavis disappeared from the quiet town of Cinceria. No answers, no trace, no goodbye. A year later she was declared dead, and Yunis left town rather than face it.

02 Today

Coming home

Yunis returns to find Cinceria hollowed out. Half the town has left, the rest whisper that it is cursed, and a gang has made the streets their own. Waiting for him are the friends he left behind: Estella, Angie and Rictor.

03 That night

One last goodbye

Together again, the four make their way into the forest, to the memorial they built for June. They are ready to finally lay their grief to rest. Yunis asks her for just one thing: a sign.

04 Then

Something answers.

They wake somewhere that is not Cinceria. A world called Iolade, a prophecy they were never meant to read, and six months before fate is sealed for good.

04 Combat

The STANCE system

Within Time's battles run on STANCE, a fully original take on turn-based combat. Up to four party members fight at once, and the turn order is always in view, so every encounter comes down to reading what happens next and planning around it.

Wear an enemy down until they break and get knocked out of the order entirely. Hunt for weak points, team up for combo attacks, and when the moment is right, commit your whole party to STANCE and hit harder together.

Every party member fights in their own way, so the team you bring changes how each battle plays out.
